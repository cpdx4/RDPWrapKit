RDP shows "Not Listening" but the service is Running

Fix

The Show RDP Info page reports RDP is Not Listening on port 3389, even though the Remote Desktop service is listed as Running. Fixed with an in-place Windows repair that keeps your apps and data.

View fix

My game controller is moving in RDP

Fix

Your game controller is moving your character inside RDP when you don't want it to. RDP thinks you're trying to use the controller within it. Hide the controller from RDP with the HidHide app while keeping it available to your games.
