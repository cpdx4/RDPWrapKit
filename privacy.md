# Privacy Policy

**RDPWrapKit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

---

## What happens during installation

During setup, the installer may download files from the following sources to install required components:

- **GitHub** — installer assets and release files
- **Microsoft** — Visual C++ Redistributables and Remote Desktop software

These connections are made solely to complete installation. No personal data is sent to any server.

---

## What we don't do

- No telemetry
- No analytics
- No tracking of any kind
- No data collection before, during, or after installation
